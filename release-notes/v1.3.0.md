### What's new
- **Headphone battery.** When your earbuds or headphones connect, the island shows their battery with an animated illustration. Tap it for the card with left, right and case levels when the headphones report them, and a warning when they run low.
- **Rides and deliveries.** Uber, DiDi, inDrive, Cabify, Lyft, Rappi, Uber Eats, DoorDash, iFood, Amazon, Mercado Libre and more show the arrival time in the island. The card has a route with the stages and the vehicle moving along it, the driver and the plate. It has its own illustrations for rides, food and packages. Everything is read from the app's notification, on the phone.
- **Camera and microphone dot.** A green dot in the island while any app uses the camera or the microphone. No new permissions.
- **Calendar in At a glance.** Your next event from Google Calendar, with a countdown, a **Join Meet** button for video calls and a reminder 30 minutes before. It is read on the phone and never leaves it. It needs calendar access, asked only when you turn the view on.
- **Finance removed from At a glance.** The app no longer contacts Yahoo Finance or Google's logo service. Your other settings are kept.

Updating: open Material Island and tap **Update** on the card (or **About → Check for updates**), or install the APK over the old one. Your settings are kept.

Two APKs: `material-island-1.3.0.apk` is the usual version; `material-island-fdroid-1.3.0.apk` is the same app without the updater, for F-Droid or Obtainium.
