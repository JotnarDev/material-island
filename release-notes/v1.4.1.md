### What's new
- **Quick Settings tile.** Pause or resume the island from the Quick Settings panel. While it's paused, Android's own pop-up notifications come back. Material Island offers to add the tile from its home screen.
- **Sunrise and sunset** in the At a glance weather card, as an extra column when they fall within the hours shown.
- **11 more leagues** in At a glance → Sports: Europa League, Conference League, Eredivisie, Primeira Liga, Copa Libertadores, Copa Sudamericana, and the leagues of Argentina, Brazil, Colombia, Chile and Peru.
- **°F or °C from your region.** The first time you turn on At a glance, the unit follows your phone's region. A unit you already picked is kept.
- New screenshots in the README and the store listings.

Updating: open Material Island and tap **Update** on the card (or **About → Check for updates**), or install the APK over the old one. Your settings are kept.

Two APKs: `material-island-1.4.1.apk` is the usual version; `material-island-fdroid-1.4.1.apk` is the same app without the updater, for F-Droid or Obtainium.
