### What's new
- **In Spanish.** The whole app speaks Spanish when your phone is in Spanish. You can also pick the language for Material Island alone in Android's settings (**Apps → Material Island → Language**).
- **Setup guide.** On first launch a short wizard walks you through each permission, one step at a time, and moves on by itself when you come back with it turned on. You can open it again in **About → Setup guide**.
- **Liquid bar.** A new open style: at rest a black bar covers the status bar from edge to edge, with a Pixel-style clock, battery, signal and notification icons. When something happens it shrinks into the camera and a drop falls out of it. In **Bar items** you choose what it shows: network speed, battery temperature, RAM, alarm, Do Not Disturb, sound mode, Bluetooth, airplane mode, Wi-Fi, mobile signal and battery percentage.
- **Speakers and laptops.** Bluetooth speakers and computers now get their own animated illustration when they connect, with the speaker's battery when it reports it.

Updating: open Material Island and tap **Update** on the card (or **About → Check for updates**), or install the APK over the old one. Your settings are kept.

Two APKs: `material-island-1.4.0.apk` is the usual version; `material-island-fdroid-1.4.0.apk` is the same app without the updater, for F-Droid or Obtainium.
